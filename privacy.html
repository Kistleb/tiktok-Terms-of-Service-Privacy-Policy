Privacy Policy

VK Video Planner

Last updated: 24 October 2026

1. Scope

This policy describes how the operator of VK Video Planner handles information when using the tool. The tool is intended to be operated locally by its owner. Update this policy if you make it available to other users or change how it processes data.

2. Information processed

Depending on configuration and use, the tool may process video URLs submitted by the operator, downloaded video files, captions, scheduled publication times, application logs, Telegram user identifiers and messages used to operate the bot, and authentication tokens or API credentials for connected services.

3. Purpose and storage

Queue information and downloaded files may be stored locally on the operator's computer in a SQLite database and local folders. Telegram messages are processed to operate the bot. When enabled, information needed for video publishing may be sent to the relevant third-party platform. Optional AI caption services may receive text sent to them for caption generation.

4. Third-party services

Telegram, TikTok, VK, AI providers, and hosting providers (if any) process information under their own privacy policies and terms. Review those policies before connecting an account or enabling a provider. This tool's operator does not control third-party processing.

5. Sharing and sale

The operator does not intend to sell personal information. Information may be transmitted to a third-party service when necessary for a feature explicitly configured by the operator, or where required by law.

6. Retention and security

Local queue records, logs, and downloaded files may remain on the operator's computer until deleted or cleaned up. Keep credentials private, restrict access to the computer and configuration files, and revoke credentials that may have been exposed. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Your choices

The operator can stop the tool, remove locally stored records and files, and disconnect third-party integrations. Requests about information handled by a third-party service may need to be made directly to that service.

8. Children

This tool is not designed to collect information from children.
